## Rationale
P4283, "Requires Clauses for Contract Assertions": a contract may carry a
`requires` clause, so that in a template the assertion applies only to the
instantiations whose constraints it satisfies.

Most of the work is parsing, and it is parsing under an ambiguity the
ordinary constraint grammar does not have.  `ParseConstraintLogicalAndExpression`
grows an `IsContractRequiresClause` flag for one reason, recorded at the
site: in a contract, a `[` following the constraint introduces **P3098's
postcondition captures**, not a subscript.  Without the flag the recovery
path swallows the capture list into the constraint and the diagnostics that
follow describe neither construct.

`LateParseContractRequiresClause` handles the deferred case, and lives in
`ParseContracts.cpp` alongside the base late-parsing machinery that
`1040-p2900-base-parse` claims.

`TreeTransform.h` is the instantiation side -- the clause has to be
substituted with the contract, and `1080-p2900-base-sema-templates`'s
transform does not know about it.

**`contract_control(...)` as an expression is here**, in
`ParseCastExpression`: it evaluates its operand with
`InAssertionControlExpression` set, which is how a P3400 label expression
is written in a context that would otherwise resolve names differently.
That it lands in P4283's commit rather than P3400's follows the mapping's
rule about where a shared hunk goes, not a claim about which paper owns it.

## Compile gap
Needs `1040-p2900-base-parse` for the late-parsing machinery,
`1080-p2900-base-sema-templates` for the transform it extends,
`4000-p3098` for the capture list the disambiguation is against, and
`4200-p3400-core` for `contract_control`'s keyword.  All precede it.

None outward.

**Behaviour trap:** with the flag off the syntax is diagnosed.  With the
flag on and the `IsContractRequiresClause` disambiguation absent, a
contract carrying both a requires clause and postcondition captures parses
*something* -- badly, and with errors pointing at the wrong construct.
That is the case `matrix-sfinae-p4283*` in `4650-cross-feature-matrix`
exists to pin.
